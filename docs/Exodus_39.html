出エジプト記 39Exodus 39

1
彼らは青色、紫色、緋色の撚り糸から、聖所で仕えるための編んだ装束を作った。彼らは主がモーセに命じられたとおりに、アロンのための聖なる装束を作った。
From the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn they made woven garments for serving in the sanctuary; they made holy garments that were for Aaron, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

2

He made the ephod of gold, blue, purple, scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen.
3
彼らは金を薄い板に打ち延ばし、それを細い紐状に切り刻んで、意匠家の細工として、青色、紫色、緋色の撚り糸や上等の亜麻布の中に織り込んだ。
They hammered the gold into thin sheets and cut it into narrow strips to weave them into the blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and into the fine linen, the work of an artistic designer.

4
彼らはエポデのために、その二つの端に取り付けられた肩掛けを作り、それによってつなぎ合わされるようにした。
They made shoulder pieces for it, attached to two of its corners, so it could be joined together.
5
エポデの上にあってそれを締める巧みに織られた帯は、エポデと同じように作られ、エポデと一続きのものであり、主がモーセに命じられたとおり、金糸、青色、紫色、緋色の撚り糸、撚り糸で織った上等の亜麻布で作られた。
The artistically woven waistband of the ephod that was on it was like it, of one piece with it, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and fine twisted linen, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

6

They set the onyx stones in gold filigree settings, engraved as with the engravings of a seal with the names of the sons of Israel.

7
彼は主がモーセに命じられたとおり、イスラエル人のための記念の石として、それらをエポデの肩掛けの上に置いた。
He put them on the shoulder pieces of the ephod as stones of memorial for the Israelites, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

8
彼は胸当てを意匠家の細工として、エポデと同じ仕立てで、金糸、青色、紫色、緋色の撚り糸、撚り糸で織った上等の亜麻布で作った。
He made the breastpiece, the work of an artistic designer, in the same fashion as the ephod, of gold, blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, and fine twisted linen.

胸当ては四角形であった。彼らはそれを二重にして作り、二重にした状態で長さ九インチ、幅九インチであった。
It was square—they made the breastpiece doubled, nine inches long and nine inches wide when doubled.
10
彼らはその上に宝石を四列はめ込んだ。第一列はルビー、トパーズ、緑柱石。
They set on it four rows of stones: a row with a ruby, a topaz, and a beryl—the first row;

and the third row, a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst;

and the fourth row, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a jasper. They were enclosed in gold filigree settings.
14
これらの石はイスラエルの子らの名を表す十二個で、その名の数に対応していた。十二部族のそれぞれに対応する各々の名は、印章を彫るように刻まれていた。
The stones were for the names of the sons of Israel, twelve, corresponding to the number of their names. Each name corresponding to one of the twelve tribes was like the engravings of a seal.

and they made two gold filigree settings and two gold rings, and they attached the two rings to the upper two ends of the breastpiece.

They attached the two gold chains to the two rings at the ends of the breastpiece;

その二本の鎖のもう一方の端を二つの飾り座に取り付け、エポデの前にある肩掛けの上に取り付けた。
the other two ends of the two chains they attached to the two settings, and they attached them to the shoulder pieces of the ephod at the front of it.

They made two rings of gold and put them on the other two ends of the breastpiece on its edge, which is on the inner side of the ephod.

彼らはさらに二つの金の環を作り、エポデの前にある二つの肩掛けの下部、エポデの帯の上の継ぎ目に近いところに取り付けた。
They made two more gold rings and attached them to the bottom of the two shoulder pieces on the front of the ephod, close to the juncture above the waistband of the ephod.

21
彼らは主がモーセに命じられたとおり、胸当てがエポデの帯の上に位置し、エポデから外れないように、青色の撚り紐によって胸当ての環をエポデの環に結びつけた。
They tied the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod by blue cord, so that it was above the waistband of the ephod, so that the breastpiece would not be loose from the ephod, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

He made the robe of the ephod completely blue, the work of a weaver.

上着の中央には襟ぐりのような開口部があり、それが破れないように開口部の周囲に縁が付けられていた。
There was an opening in the center of the robe, like the opening of a collar, with an edge all around the opening so that it could not be torn.

They made pomegranates of blue, purple, and scarlet yarn and twisted linen around the hem of the robe.

There was a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, all around the hem of the robe, to be used in ministering, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

The sash was of fine twisted linen and blue, purple, and scarlet yarn, the work of an embroiderer, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

They attached to it a blue cord to attach it to the turban above, just as the Lord had commanded Moses.

So all the work of the tabernacle, the tent of meeting, was completed, and the Israelites did according to all that the Lord had commanded Moses—they did it exactly so.

NET Notes (翻訳者注)

1:1
[解説] この章も、途中でいくらかの変更はあるものの、先に与えられた指示とほぼ同一の内容となる。
[解説] This chapter also will be almost identical to the instructions given earlier, with a few changes along the way.
3:1
[翻訳注] 動詞は「行う、細工する」を意味する不定詞である。直訳すると「それらを青色の中に細工する」となる。おそらく織り込みや刺繍を意図している。
[翻訳注] The verb is the infinitive that means “to do, to work.” It could be given a literal rendering: “to work [them into] the blue….” Weaving or embroidering is probably what is intended.
5:1
[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「それから」、すなわち同じものからの一続きを意味する。
[翻訳注] Heb “from it” or the same.
6:1
[翻訳注] または「印章が彫られるように」。
[翻訳注] Or “as seals are engraved.”
6:2
[解説] 十二の名はイスラエルの子らの名であった。これは十二人の息子そのものを記念するのではなく、その名を冠した十二部族を記念することを意図している。
[解説] The twelve names were those of Israel’s sons. The idea was not the remembrance of the twelve sons as such, but the twelve tribes that bore their names.

24:1
[翻訳注] この語は単に「撚り合わされた」あるいは「撚られた」を意味する。これらの一覧に頻出する撚り糸で織った亜麻布を指している可能性もあれば、撚り合わされた糸を指している可能性もある。七十人訳（LXX）は「撚り糸で織った上等の亜麻布」と読んでいる。これはサマリア五書と七十人訳を除き、出エジプト記28章33節の本文には見られない。
[翻訳注] The word is simply “twined” or “twisted.” It may refer to the twisted linen that so frequently is found in these lists; or, it may refer to the yarn twisted. The LXX reads “fine twined linen.” This is not found in the text of Exod 28:33, except in Smr and LXX.
26:1
[翻訳注] 「〜があった」という言葉は文体上の理由から補訳されている。
[翻訳注] The words “there was” are supplied in the translation for stylistic reasons.

[翻訳注] 「仕える」という不定詞が存在し、「用いるための」は文脈から補足されている。
[翻訳注] The infinitive “to minister” is present; “to be used” is supplied from the context.
32:1
[解説] この書の結びの部分は、複数の主題を一つにまとめて完全な締めくくりへと導く。それは単に幕屋の完成にとどまらず、この書の冒頭で示された神の計画、すなわちご自身の民とともに住まわれるという計画の成就でもある。
[解説] The last sections of the book bring several themes together to a full conclusion. Not only is it the completion of the tabernacle, it is the fulfillment of God’s plan revealed at the beginning of the book, i.e., to reside with his people.

[翻訳注] The form is the infinitive construct; it means the clothes to be used “to minister” in the holy place.
43:1
[翻訳注] または「調べた」（NASB、TEV）、NCV「綿密に見た」。
[翻訳注] Or “examined” (NASB, TEV); NCV “looked closely at.”
43:2
[翻訳注] 提示の不変化詞は、読者にモーセの視点と彼の喜びの感覚を与えるように、彼が見たものに注意を向けさせている。「そして見よ、彼らは……」。
[翻訳注] The deictic particle draws attention to what he saw in such a way as to give the reader Moses’ point of view and a sense of his pleasure: “and behold, they…”
43:3
[解説] 出エジプト記39章43節の状況と言葉遣いは、人々を祝福することや造られたものを検分するというモチーフにおいて、創世記1章28節および31節を想起させる。
[解説] The situation and wording in Exod 39:43 are reminiscent of Gen 1:28 and 31, with the motifs of blessing people and inspecting what has been made.
