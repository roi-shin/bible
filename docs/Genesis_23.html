[翻訳注] Heb “And the years of Sarah were one hundred years and twenty years and seven years, the years of the life of Sarah.”
2:1
[翻訳注] ヘブル語では「サラ」。文体の理由から、翻訳では固有名詞を代名詞（「彼女」）に置き換えている。
[翻訳注] Heb “Sarah.” The proper name has been replaced in the translation by the pronoun (“she”) for stylistic reasons.
2:2
[解説] 149-150頁）。これらの儀礼は遺体の前、おそらくサラの天幕の中で行われたと考えられる。こうしてアブラハムは喪に服すために入り、それから死者を葬るために立ち上がった（3節）。
149-50). They would have been carried out in the presence of the corpse, probably in Sarah’s tent. So Abraham came in to mourn; then he rose up to go and bury his dead (v. 3).
3:1
[翻訳注] ヘブル語では「そしてアブラハムはその死者の顔の前から立ち上がった」。
[翻訳注] Heb “And Abraham arose from upon the face of his dead.”
3:2
[翻訳注] ここでヘブル語の「ヘテ」を「ヒッタイト人」と訳す者もいるが（5、7、10、16、18、20節も同様）、これではこれらの人々がアナトリアの古典的ヒッタイト人であるかのような印象を与える。しかし、カナン人のグループとみられるこのヘテの子ら（創世記10:15参照）と小アジアのヒッタイト人との間には、知られている関連性はない。H. A. Hoffner, Jr., “Hittites,” Peoples of the Old Testament World, 152-53 を参照。
[翻訳注] Some translate the Hebrew term “Heth” as “Hittites” here (also in vv. 5, 7, 10, 16, 18, 20), but this gives the impression that these people were the classical Hittites of Anatolia. However, there is no known connection between these sons of Heth, apparently a Canaanite group (see Gen 10:15), and the Hittites of Asia Minor. See H. A. Hoffner, Jr., “Hittites,” Peoples of the Old Testament World, 152-53.
4:1
[翻訳注] ヘブル語では「居留外国人であり定住者」。

[翻訳注] Heb “give,” which is used here as an idiom for “sell” (see v. 9). The idiom reflects the polite bartering that was done in the culture at the time.

[翻訳注] Following the imperative, the cohortative with the prefixed conjunction expresses purpose.

Gottstein, “Nasi’ ‘elohim (Gen 23:6),” VT 3 (1953) 298-99; and D. W. Thomas, “Consideration of Some Unusual Ways of Expressing the Superlative in Hebrew,” VT 3 (1953) 215-16.
