He launched a military campaign against the king of the Ammonites and defeated them. That year the Ammonites paid him 100 talents of silver, 10,000 cors of wheat, and 10,000 cors of barley. The Ammonites also paid this same amount of annual tribute the next two years.

5:2
[翻訳注] ヘブル語の言葉キカール（「円」の意）は、一般に丸いものを指す。金属について用いられる場合、その金属で作られた円盤状の分銅、転じて標準的な重量単位を指す。古い（バビロニアの）基準では、「タラント」の重さは130ポンド（58.9kg）であったが、後に108.3ポンド（49.1kg）に引き下げられた。近年の研究によると、「軽量」基準のタラントは67.3ポンド（30.6kg）であったとされる。これを計算の基準とすると、銀の重量は6,730ポンド（3,060kg）となる。
[翻訳注] The Hebrew word כִּכַּר (kikar, “circle”) refers generally to something that is round. When used of metals it can refer to a disk-shaped weight made of the metal or, by extension, to a standard unit of weight. According to the older (Babylonian) standard the “talent” weighed 130 lbs. (58.9 kg), but later this was lowered to 108.3 lbs. (49.1 kg). More recent research suggests the “light” standard talent was 67.3 lbs. (30.6 kg). Using this as the standard for calculation, the weight of the silver was 6,730 lbs. (3,060 kg).
5:3
[解説] 乾量の単位としてのコルは、おおむね6ブッシェル（約220リットル）に相当した。
[解説] As a unit of dry measure a kor was roughly equivalent to six bushels (about 220 liters).
5:4
[翻訳注] ヘブル語本文は「小麦1万コル、大麦1万」。ヘブル語本文では大麦の測定単位が省略されているが、小麦の単位と同様に「コル」であると理解される。
[翻訳注] Heb “10,000 kors of wheat and 10,000 of barley.” The unit of measure of the barley is omitted in the Hebrew text, but is understood to be “kors,” the same as the measures of wheat.

[翻訳注] Heb “lay down with his fathers.”
9:2

[解説] The phrase the City of David refers here to the fortress of Zion in Jerusalem, not to Bethlehem. See 2 Sam 5:7.
