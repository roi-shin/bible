歴代誌上 101 Chronicles 10

1
ペリシテ人はイスラエルと戦い、イスラエルびとはペリシテ人の前を逃げ、多くの者が討たれてギルボア山で倒れた。
Now the Philistines fought against Israel. The Israelites fled before the Philistines and many of them fell dead on Mount Gilboa.
2
ペリシテ人はサウルとその息子たちを激しく追撃し、サウルの息子ヨナタン、アビナダブ、マルキ・シュアを打ち殺した。
The Philistines stayed right on the heels of Saul and his sons. They struck down Saul’s sons Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malki-Shua.

4
サウルは自分の武器持ちの若者に言った、「剣を抜いて私を刺し貫きなさい。さもないと、この割礼のない者どもがやって来て私をなぶり者にするだろう」。しかし、武器持ちは非常に恐れてそれをしようとしなかった。そこでサウルは自ら剣を取ってその上に身を投げた。
Saul told his armor-bearer, “Draw your sword and stab me with it. Otherwise these uncircumcised people will come and torture me.” But his armor-bearer refused to do it because he was very afraid. So Saul took the sword and fell on it.

When all the Israelites who were in the valley saw that the army had fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they abandoned their cities and fled. The Philistines came and occupied them.

8
翌日、ペリシテ人が死体から戦利品を剥ぎ取りに来たところ、サウルとその息子たちがギルボア山で倒れているのを発見した。
The next day, when the Philistines came to strip loot from the corpses, they discovered Saul and his sons lying dead on Mount Gilboa.
9
彼らはサウルの死体から剥ぎ取り、その首と武具を持ち去った。そして、自分たちの偶像と民にその知らせを告げるため、ペリシテ人の全地に使いを送った。
They stripped his corpse and then carried off his head and his armor. They sent messengers throughout the land of the Philistines proclaiming the news to their idols and their people.
10
彼らはサウルの武具を自分たちの神々の神殿に置き、その首をダゴンの神殿にさらした。
They placed his armor in the temple of their gods and hung his head in the temple of Dagon.

all the warriors went and recovered the bodies of Saul and his sons and brought them to Jabesh. They buried their remains under the oak tree in Jabesh and fasted for seven days.

So Saul died because he was unfaithful to the Lord and did not obey the Lord’s instructions; he even tried to conjure up underworld spirits.

6:1
[翻訳注] ヘブル語では「彼の全家」。これはおそらく一般的な総括的陳述として理解されるべきである。サムエル記上31章6節を参照すると、3人の息子以外にもサウルの世帯の他の男性が含まれていた可能性がある。王位を継承するはずであった彼の世帯の男性メンバーのみを指す場合であっても（NLTの「彼の王朝を終わりに導いた」のように）、ここにも例外が存在する。なぜなら、サウルの息子のひとりエシュバアル（サムエル記下2面8節では「イシュボシェテ」）は戦死せず、レカブとバアナによって暗殺されるまで（サムエル記下4章5-6節）2年間イスラエルの王として治めたからである（サムエル記下2章10節）。ユダ族は決してイシュボシェテを王として認めず、代わりにダビデに従った（サムエル記下2章10節）。
[翻訳注] Heb “all his house.” This is probably to be understood as a general summary statement. It could include other males in Saul’s household besides his three sons, cf. 1 Sam 31:6. If it refers only to the male members of his household who would have stood in succession to the throne (cf. NLT, “bringing his dynasty to an end,”) even here there is an exception, since one of Saul’s sons, Eshbaal (or “Ishbosheth” in 2 Sam 2:8) was not killed in the battle and became king over Israel, which he ruled for two years (2 Sam 2:10) until he was assassinated by Rechab and Baanah (2 Sam 4:5-6). The tribe of Judah never acknowledged Ishbosheth as king; instead they followed David (2 Sam 2:10).

[翻訳注] Heb “and Saul died because of his unfaithfulness by which he acted unfaithfully against the Lord, concerning the word of the Lord which he did not keep, also to Saul, a ritual pit to seek.” The text alludes to the incident recorded in . The Hebrew term אוֹב (’ov, “ritual pit”) refers to a pit used by a magician to conjure up underworld spirits. In 1 Sam 28:7 the witch of Endor is called a בַּעֲלַת־אוֹב (ba’alat-’ov, “owner of a ritual pit”). See H. A. Hoffner, “Second Millennium Antecedents to the Hebrew áo‚b,” JBL 86 (1967): 385-401.
