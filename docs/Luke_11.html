[解説] ユダヤ教において、死者や死者に関連するものにそれと知らずに触れることさえも人を汚れた者とする（民数記19:11-22、レビ記21:1-3、ミシュナ・デマイ2:3）。儀式的な汚れを被ることに敏感であったパリサイ人にとって、自分たちが汚れの原因となっていると告げられることは非常に痛烈な叱責であった。

To Pharisees, who would have been so sensitive about contracting ceremonial uncleanness, it would have been quite a stinging rebuke to be told they caused it.

[翻訳注] ギリシア語では「あなたがた自身は触れない」。これは次の二つのうちの一つを意味し得る。(1) 彼ら自身が（様々な専門的例外規定を通じて）行わないことを他人に課しているか、あるいは(2) 他人が要求されていることを果たすのを助けようと少しも努力しないかである。これらの宗教指導者たちが律法に払っていたとされる細心の注意を考慮すると、第二の選択肢のほうが可能性が高い（L&N 18.11参照）。
[翻訳注] Grk “you yourselves do not touch.” This could mean one of two things: (1) Either they make others do what they themselves do not (through various technical exceptions) or (2) they make no effort to help the others fulfill what they are required to do. Considering the care these religious figures are said to have given to the law, the second option is more likely (see L&N 18.11).

[解説] The effect of what the experts in the law were doing was to deny the message of the prophets and thus honor their death by supporting those who had sought their removal. The charge that this is what previous generations did shows the problem is chronic. As T. W. Manson said, the charge here is “The only prophet you honor is a dead prophet!” (The Sayings of Jesus, 101).
