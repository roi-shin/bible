[写本注] The Hebrew text adds, “with their hands to their mouths,” This makes no sense in light of v. 5, which distinguishes between dog-like lappers (who would not use their hands to drink) and those who kneel (who would use their hands). It seems likely that the words “with their hands to their mouths” have been misplaced from v. 6. They fit better at the end of v. 5 or v. 6. Perhaps these words were originally a marginal scribal note which was later accidentally inserted into the text in the wrong place.

[写本注] MT has “and throughout the camp,” but the conjunction (“and”) is due to dittography and should be dropped. Compare the ancient versions, which lack the conjunction here.

25:1
[解説] オレブとゼエブという名前は、それぞれ「カラス」と「狼」を意味し、ミデアン人たちがイスラエルにとってハイエナや掠奪者のようであったことにふさわしい名前です。
[解説] The names Oreb and Zeeb, which mean “Raven” and “Wolf” respectively, are appropriate because the Midianites had been like scavengers and predators to Israel.

[翻訳注] ヘブル語本文は動詞「殺害した」を繰り返していますが、文体上の理由から翻訳では繰り返していません。
[翻訳注] The Hebrew text repeats the verb “executed.” This has not been repeated in the translation for stylistic reasons.
