4

Look, I am ready to build a temple to honor the Lord my God and to dedicate it to him in order to burn fragrant incense before him, to set out the bread that is regularly displayed, and to offer burnt sacrifices each morning and evening, and on Sabbaths, new moon festivals, and at other times appointed by the Lord our God. This is something Israel must do on a permanent basis.

私が建てようとしている神殿は壮大である。私たちの神はすべての神々にまさって大いなる方だからである。
I will build a great temple, for our God is greater than all gods.
6
天も天の頂も主を収めることができないのに、だれが主のために神殿を建てることができようか。主のために神殿を建てるなど、私はいったい何者であろうか。それは主の前でいけにえをささげる場所にすぎない。
Of course, who can really build a temple for him, since the sky and the highest heavens cannot contain him? Who am I that I should build him a temple! It will really be only a place to offer sacrifices before him.

1:1
[解説] 2章1節から2章18節までの英語聖書の節番号はヘブル語本文（BHS）の節番号と異なっており、英語訳2章1節＝ヘブル語本文1章18節、英語訳2章2節＝ヘブル語本文2章1節、英語訳2章3節＝ヘブル語本文2章2節などとなり、英語訳2章18節＝ヘブル語本文2章17節まで続く。3章1節以降は、英語訳とヘブル語本文の節番号は一致する。
[解説] Beginning with 2:1, the verse numbers through 2:18 in the English Bible differ from the verse numbers in the Hebrew text (BHS), with 2:1 ET = 1:18 HT, 2:2 ET = 2:1 HT, 2:3 ET = 2:2 HT, etc., through 2:18 ET = 2:17 HT. Beginning with 3:1 the verse numbers in the ET and HT are again the same.

[翻訳注] Heb “20,000 baths” (also a second time later in this verse). A bath was a liquid measure roughly equivalent to six gallons (about 22 liters), so this was a quantity of about 120,000 gallons (440,000 liters).
