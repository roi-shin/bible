In 20:5 the town leaders are implicated in the crime, suggesting that all the men of the city were involved. If so, the implication is that the entire male population of the town were good-for-nothings.

26:1
ここで「主人」と訳されているヘブライ語の用語は複数形である。この複数形はここでは程度を示し、女性に対するレビ人の絶対的な主権を強調している。
[翻訳注] The Hebrew term here translated “master,” is plural. The plural indicates degree here and emphasizes the Levite’s absolute sovereignty over the woman.

[翻訳注] Heb “The woman came at the turning of the morning and fell at the door of the house of the man where her master was until the light.”

30:3
（元の）七十人訳のアレクサンドリア写本（A）には以下の追加の言葉がある：「そして彼は自分が遣わした男たちに指示して言った。『このようにあなた方はすべてのイスラエル人の男に言うべきである：「イスラエル人がエジプトを出た日から今日に至るまで、このようなことは決してなかった」』」
[写本注] Codex Alexandrinus (A) of the (original) LXX has the following additional words: “And he instructed the men whom he sent out, ‘Thus you will say to every male Israelite: “There has never been anything like this from the day the Israelites left Egypt till the present day.”’”
