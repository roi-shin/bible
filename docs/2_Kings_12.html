エフウの第7年にエホアシュが王となり、エルサレムで40年間治めた。彼の母はベエル・シェバ出身のツィビアであった。
(12:2) In Jehu’s seventh year Jehoash became king; he reigned for 40 years in Jerusalem. His mother was Zibiah, who was from Beer Sheba.

2
エホアシュは、祭司エホヤダから教えを受けていた間、その生涯を通じて主が良しとされることを行った。
Jehoash did what the Lord approved all his days when Jehoiada the priest taught him.

But the high places were not eliminated; the people continued to offer sacrifices and burn incense on the high places.

Jehoash said to the priests, “I place at your disposal all the consecrated silver that has been brought to the Lord’s temple, including the silver collected from the census tax, the silver received from those who have made vows, and all the silver that people have voluntarily contributed to the Lord’s temple.

The priests should receive the silver they need from the treasurers and repair any damage to the temple they discover.”

6
しかし、エホアシュ王の治世の第23年になっても、祭司たちは宮の破損箇所を修理していなかった。
By the twenty-third year of King Jehoash’s reign the priests had still not repaired the damage to the temple.
7
そこでエホアシュ王は祭司エホヤダとほかの祭司たちを呼び寄せ、彼らに言った。「なぜあなたたちは宮の破損箇所を修理しないのか。今からは、管理している者たちから銀を受け取ってはならない。それを宮の修理のために使わないのであれば。」
So King Jehoash summoned Jehoiada the priest along with the other priests, and said to them, “Why have you not repaired the damage to the temple? Now, take no more silver from your treasurers unless you intend to use it to repair the damage.”

8
祭司たちは、民から銀を集めないこと、および宮の修理の個人的な責任を負わないことに同意した。
The priests agreed not to collect silver from the people and relieved themselves of personal responsibility for the temple repairs.

9
祭司エホヤダは箱を一つ用意し、その蓋に穴を開け、主の宮の入り口の祭壇の右側にそれを置いた。入り口を守る祭司たちは、主の宮に持ち込まれたすべての銀をその中に投げ入れた。
Jehoiada the priest took a chest and drilled a hole in its lid. He placed it on the right side of the altar near the entrance of the Lord’s temple. The priests who guarded the entrance would put into it all the silver brought to the Lord’s temple.

When they saw the chest was full of silver, the royal secretary and the high priest counted the silver that had been brought to the Lord’s temple and bagged it up.

as well as masons and stonecutters. They bought wood and chiseled stone to repair the damage to the Lord’s temple and also paid for all the other expenses.

The silver brought to the Lord’s temple was not used for silver bowls, trimming shears, basins, trumpets, or any kind of gold or silver implements.

King Jehoash of Judah collected all the sacred items that his ancestors Jehoshaphat, Jehoram, and Ahaziah, kings of Judah, had consecrated, as well as his own sacred items and all the gold that could be found in the treasuries of the Lord’s temple and the royal palace. He sent it all to King Hazael of Syria, who then withdrew from Jerusalem.

The rest of the events of Joash’s reign, including all his accomplishments, are recorded in the scroll called the Annals of the Kings of Judah.

His servants Jozabad son of Shimeath and Jehozabad son of Shomer murdered him. He was buried with his ancestors in the City of David. His son Amaziah replaced him as king.

4:2
[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「一人の男が通り過ぎることの銀」。この句の正確な意味には議論があるが、「通り過ぎる」を意味するアヴァール（'avar）は、ここでは数えることを指しており、課税目的で行われた人口調査を指していると考えられる。M. コガンおよびH. タドモア著『列王記第二』（アンカー・バイブル）137頁を参照。
[翻訳注] Heb “the silver of passing over a man.” The precise meaning of the phrase is debated, but עָבַר (’avar), “pass over,” probably refers here to counting, suggesting the reference is to a census conducted for taxation purposes. See M. Cogan and H. Tadmor, II Kings (AB), 137.
4:3
[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「人々についての銀、彼の評価」。この句の正確な意味は不確かであるが、並行箇所の表現から、ここでは個人的な誓願を指しているものと示唆される。M. コガンおよびH. タドモア著『列王記第二』（アンカー・バイブル）137頁を参照。
[翻訳注] Heb “the silver of persons, his valuation.” The precise meaning of the phrase is uncertain, but parallels in suggest that personal vows are referred to here. See M. Cogan and H. Tadmor, II Kings (AB), 137.
4:4
[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「人の心に上り、主の宮に持って行こうとするすべての銀」。
[翻訳注] Heb “all the silver which goes up on the heart of a man to bring to the house of the Lord.”
5:1
[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「祭司たちは各自、自分の管理者から受け取り、そこに見出されるすべての破損に関して、宮の破損を修理させよ」。ここで「管理者」と訳されている言葉マカール（makar）は、この箇所にのみ現れる。これが「商人」や「支援者」を意味するのではないかとする説もある。ウガリット語のテキストでは神殿の役人のリストに登場することから、この文脈では神殿の資金支出を担当していた個人を指していると考えられる。
[翻訳注] Heb “Let the priests take for themselves, each from his treasurer, and let them repair the damage of the temple, with respect to all the damage that is found there.” The word מַכָּר (makar), translated here “treasurer,” occurs only in this passage. Some suggest it means “merchant” or “benefactor.” Its usage in Ugaritic texts, where it appears in a list of temple officials, suggests that it refers in this context to individuals who were in charge of disbursing temple funds.
7:1
[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「今や、あなたたちの管理者から銀を受け取ってはならない。なぜなら宮の破損のためにそれを与えなければならないからである」。
[翻訳注] Heb “Now, do not take silver from your treasurers, because for the damages to the temple you must give it.”
8:1
[翻訳注] この箇所以外で、動詞ウート（'ut）が現れるのは創世記34章15〜22節のみである。
[翻訳注] Outside of this passage the verb אוּת (’ut) appears only in Gen 34:15-22.
8:2
[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「そして宮の破損を修理しないこと」。これは祭司たちがもはや宮の修理に関心を持たなくなったという意味ではない。後続の文脈が明らかにしているように、祭司たちは自分たちで修理しようとするのではなく、熟練した労働者を雇って宮の破損を修理することに決めたのである。
[翻訳注] Heb “and not to repair the damages to the temple.” This does not mean that the priests were no longer interested in repairing the temple. As the following context makes clear, the priests decided to hire skilled workers to repair the damage to the temple, rather than trying to make the repairs themselves.
