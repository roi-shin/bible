[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「〜の前」。
[翻訳注] Heb “in front of.”

[翻訳注] Heb “the five lords of the Philistines, the Gazaite, the Ashdodite, the Ashkelonite, the Gathite, and the Ekronite, and the Avvites.”
4:1
[翻訳注] または「テマンから」。伝統的な節の区分が示すように、この句を後に続く語句と結びつけて解釈する場合、問題が生じる。詳細な議論についてはT. C. Butler, Joshua (WBC), 146を参照。
[翻訳注] Or “from Teman.” The phrase is especially problematic if taken with what follows, as the traditional verse division suggests. For further discussion see T. C. Butler, Joshua (WBC), 146.

[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「カナン人の全土」。

[写本注] The reading “Arah” assumes a slight emendation of the Hebrew vowel pointing. The MT reads, “and a cave,” or “and Mearah” (if one understands the word as a proper noun).

[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「シドン人に属するもの」。[地図] 位置についてはMap1-A1; JP3-F3; JP4-F3を参照。

5:1

[翻訳注] Or “the entrance to Hamath.” Most modern translations take the phrase “Lebo Hamath” to be a proper name, but often provide a note with the alternative, where “Hamath” is the proper name and לְבוֹא (lÿvo’) is taken to mean “entrance to.”
