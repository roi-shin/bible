士師記 1Judges 1

1
ヨシュアが死んだ後、イスラエル人たちは主にお尋ねして言った、「我らのうち誰が、カナン人に対して真っ先に攻め上り、戦いを仕掛けるべきでしょうか」。
After Joshua died, the Israelites asked the Lord, “Who should lead the invasion against the Canaanites and launch the attack?”

2
主は答えられた、「ユダが攻め上るべきである。よく聞きなさい！ わたしは必ずこの地を彼らの手に入れ渡す」。
The Lord said, “The men of Judah should take the lead. Be sure of this! I am handing the land over to them.”
1 2
3
ユダの者たちは自分たちの兄弟であるシメオンの者たちに言った、「我々の割り当ての地に一緒に攻め上り、カナン人と戦ってくれ。そうすれば、我々も君たちの割り当ての地へ一緒に行こう」。そこでシメオンの者たちは彼らと共に進んだ。
The men of Judah said to their relatives, the men of Simeon, “Invade our allotted land with us and help us attack the Canaanites. Then we will go with you into your allotted land.” So the men of Simeon went with them.

4
ユダが攻め上ると、主はカナン人とペリジ人を彼らの手に渡された。彼らはベゼクで一万人の兵士を打ち殺した。
The men of Judah attacked, and the Lord handed the Canaanites and Perizzites over to them. They killed 10,000 men at Bezek.

5
彼らはベゼクでアドニ・ベゼクに出会い、彼と戦ってカナン人とペリジ人を打ち破った。
They met Adoni-Bezek at Bezek and fought him. They defeated the Canaanites and Perizzites.

6
アドニ・ベゼクが逃走したので、彼らはこれを追いかけて捕らえ、その親指と足の親指を切断した。
When Adoni-Bezek ran away, they chased him and captured him. Then they cut off his thumbs and big toes.
7
アドニ・ベゼクは言った、「七十人の王たちが、親指と足の親指を切断されて、私の食卓の下で食べかすをなめさせられていた。神は、私が彼らにしたとおりに、私に報い報復されたのだ」。彼らは彼をエルサレムへ連れて行き、彼はそこで死んだ。
Adoni-Bezek said, “Seventy kings, with thumbs and big toes cut off, used to lick up food scraps under my table. God has repaid me for what I did to them.” They brought him to Jerusalem, where he died.
1 2 3 4
8
ユダの者たちはエルサレムを攻撃してこれを占領し、剣をもって打ち破り、その都に火を放った。
The men of Judah attacked Jerusalem and captured it. They put the sword to it and set the city on fire.
9
その後、ユダの者たちは山地、ネゲブ、および低地に住むカナン人を攻撃するために下っていった。
Later the men of Judah went down to attack the Canaanites living in the hill country, the Negev, and the foothills.
1
10
ユダの者たちはヘブロンに住むカナン人を攻撃した（ヘブロンのかつての名はキリアト・アルバであった）。彼らはシェシャイ、アヒマン、タルマイを打ち殺した。
The men of Judah attacked the Canaanites living in Hebron. (Hebron used to be called Kiriath Arba.) They killed Sheshai, Ahiman, and Talmai.
11
そこから彼らはデビルの住民を攻撃した（デビルのかつての名はキリアト・セフェルであった）。
From there they attacked the people of Debir. (Debir used to be called Kiriath Sepher.)
1
12
カレブは言った、「キリアト・セフェルを攻撃してこれを占領する者には、私の娘アクサを妻として与えよう」。
Caleb said, “To the man who attacks and captures Kiriath Sepher I will give my daughter Achsah as a wife.”
13
カレブの弟ケナズの子オテニエルがそこを占領したので、カレブは自分の娘アクサを彼に妻として与えた。
When Othniel son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother, captured it, Caleb gave him his daughter Achsah as a wife.
1 2
14
アクサが嫁いだとき、彼女は父カレブをそそのかして畑を求めさせようとした。彼女がロバから身を投げ下ろすと、カレブは彼女に言った、「どうしたいのか」。
One time Achsah came and charmed her father so she could ask him for some land. When she got down from her donkey, Caleb said to her, “What would you like?”

16
さて、モーセのしゅうとであるケニ人の子孫たちは、ユダの人々と共にナツメヤシの町から上って行き、ユダの荒野にあるネゲブのアラドに至った。彼らはそこへ行き、ユダの人々と共に住んだ。
Now the descendants of the Kenite, Moses’ father-in-law, went up with the people of Judah from the city of date palm trees to Arad in the wilderness of Judah, located in the Negev. They went and lived with the people of Judah.
1 2 3
17
ユダは自分の兄弟であるシメオンの者たちと共に進み、ゼファテに住むカナン人を撃破して、その町を完全に滅ぼし尽くした。そのため、その町の名はホルマと呼ばれるようになった。
The men of Judah went with their brothers the men of Simeon and defeated the Canaanites living in Zephath. They wiped out Zephath. So people now call the city Hormah.

18
ユダの者たちはガザとその周辺地域、アシュケロンとその周辺地域、エクロンとその周辺地域を占領した。
The men of Judah captured Gaza, Ashkelon, Ekron, and the territory surrounding each of these cities.

19
主がユダの者たち共におられたので、彼らは山地を占有したが、海岸平野の住民を追い払うことはできなかった。彼らが鉄の戦車を持っていたからである。
The Lord was with the men of Judah. They conquered the hill country, but they could not conquer the people living in the coastal plain because they had chariots with iron-rimmed wheels.

Caleb received Hebron, just as Moses had promised. He drove out the three Anakites.

21

The men of Benjamin, however, did not conquer the Jebusites living in Jerusalem. The Jebusites live with the people of Benjamin in Jerusalem to this very day.

the spies spotted a man leaving the city. They said to him, “If you show us a secret entrance into the city, we will reward you.”

He showed them a secret entrance into the city, and they put the city to the sword. But they let the man and his extended family leave safely.

男はヘト人の地へ行き、町を建設してその名をルズと名付けた。その名は今日に至るまで続いている。
He moved to Hittite country and built a city. He named it Luz, and it has kept that name to this very day.

27
マナセはベト・シャンとその周辺の村々の住民を追い払わず、タアナクとその周辺の村々の住民も追い払わなかった。また、ドル、イブレアム、メギド、およびそれらの周辺の村々に住む住民をも追い払わなかった。カナン人はどうしてもその地域にとどまり続けた。
The men of Manasseh did not conquer Beth Shean, Taanach, or their surrounding towns. Nor did they conquer the people living in Dor, Ibleam, Megiddo, or their surrounding towns. The Canaanites managed to remain in those areas.

Whenever Israel was strong militarily, they forced the Canaanites to do hard labor, but they never totally conquered them.

The men of Ephraim did not conquer the Canaanites living in Gezer. The Canaanites lived among them in Gezer.

The men of Zebulun did not conquer the people living in Kitron and Nahalol. The Canaanites lived among them and were forced to do hard labor.

The men of Asher did not conquer the people living in Acco or Sidon, nor did they conquer Ahlab, Achzib, Helbah, Aphek, or Rehob.

The people of Asher live among the Canaanites residing in the land because they did not conquer them.

The men of Naphtali did not conquer the people living in Beth Shemesh or Beth Anath. They live among the Canaanites residing in the land. The Canaanites living in Beth Shemesh and Beth Anath were forced to do hard labor for them.

The Amorites managed to remain in Har Heres, Aijalon, and Shaalbim. Whenever the tribe of Joseph was strong militarily, the Amorites were forced to do hard labor.

NET Notes (翻訳者注)

1:1
[翻訳注] The Hebrew verb translated “asked” (שָׁאַל, sha’al) refers here to consulting the Lord through a prophetic oracle; cf. NAB “consulted.”
1:2
[翻訳注] Heb “Who should first go up for us against the Canaanites to attack them?”
2:1
[翻訳注] Heb “Judah should go up.”
2:2
[翻訳注] The Hebrew exclamation הִנֵּה (hinneh, traditionally, “Behold”), translated “Be sure of this,” draws attention to the following statement. The verb form in the following statement (a Hebrew perfect, indicating completed action from the standpoint of the speaker) emphasizes the certainty of the event. Though it had not yet taken place, the Lord speaks of it as a “done deal.”
3:1
[翻訳注] Heb “Judah said to Simeon, his brother.”
3:2
[翻訳注] Heb “Come up with me into our allotted land and let us attack the Canaanites.”
3:3
[翻訳注] Heb “I.” The Hebrew pronoun is singular, agreeing with the collective singular “Judah” earlier in the verse. English style requires a plural pronoun here, however.
4:1
[翻訳注] Heb “Judah went up.”
5:1
[翻訳注] Or “found.”
7:1
[翻訳注] Elsewhere this verb usually carries the sense of “to gather; to pick up; to glean,” but “lick up” seems best here in light of the peculiar circumstances described by Adoni-Bezek.
7:2
[翻訳注] The words “food scraps” are not in the Hebrew text, but are implied.
7:3
[翻訳注] Heb “Just as I did, so God has repaid me.” Note that the phrase “to them” has been supplied in the translation to clarify what is meant.

11:1
[翻訳注] Heb “they went from there against the inhabitants of Debir.” The LXX reads the verb as “they went up,” which suggests that the Hebrew text translated by the LXX read וַיַּעַל (vayya’al) rather than the MT’s וַיֵּלֶךְ (vayyelekh). It is possible that this is the text to be preferred in v. 11. Cf. Josh 15:15.
13:1
[翻訳注] “Caleb’s younger brother” may refer to Othniel or to Kenaz (in which case Othniel was Caleb’s nephew; so CEV).
13:2
[翻訳注] Heb “he”; the referent (Caleb) has been specified in the translation for clarity.
14:1
[翻訳注] Heb “she”; the referent (Acsah) has been specified in the translation for clarity.
14:2
[翻訳注] Heb “him.” The pronoun could refer to Othniel, in which case one would translate, “she incited him [Othniel] to ask her father for a field.” This is problematic, however, for Acsah, not Othniel, makes the request in v. 15. The LXX has “he [Othniel] urged her to ask her father for a field.” This appears to be an attempt to reconcile the apparent inconsistency and probably does not reflect the original text. If Caleb is understood as the referent of the pronoun, the problem disappears. For a fuller discussion of the issue, see P. G. Mosca, “Who Seduced Whom? A Note on Joshua 15:18 // Judges 1:14,” CBQ 46 (1984): 18-22. The translation takes Caleb to be the referent, specified as “her father.”
15:1
[翻訳注] Elsewhere the Hebrew word בְרָכָה (vÿrakhah) is often translated “blessing,” but here it refers to a gift (as in Gen 33:11; 1 Sam 25:27; 30:26; and 2 Kgs 5:15).
15:2
[翻訳注] Some translations regard the expressions “springs of water” (גֻּלֹּת מָיִם, gullot mayim) and “springs” (גֻּלֹּת) as place names here (cf. NRSV).
