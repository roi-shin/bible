A long time passed after the Lord made Israel secure from all their enemies, and Joshua was very old.

But in the same way that every faithful promise the Lord your God made to you has been realized, it is just as certain that if you disobey, then the Lord will bring on you every judgment until he destroys you from this good land that the Lord your God gave you.

If you violate the covenantal laws of the Lord your God which he commanded you to keep, and you follow, worship, and bow down to other gods, then the Lord will be very angry with you and you will disappear quickly from the good land that he gave to you.”

[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「大海」。これは地中海を指す典型的な表現である。
[翻訳注] Heb “the Great Sea,” the typical designation for the Mediterranean Sea.

[翻訳注] ヘブル語原文は「ヨルダン川から、わたしが断ち滅ぼしたすべての国々、そして日の沈む大海に至るまで」。すでに征服された国々に言及する後半部分と、「残された国々」に言及する前半部分との関係は解釈が難しい。
[翻訳注] Heb “from the Jordan and all the nations which I cut off and the Great Sea [at] the place where the sun sets.” The relationship of the second half of the verse, which mentions nations already conquered, to the first half, which speaks of “remaining nations,” is difficult to understand.
