[写本注] The translation follows the Qere and many medieval Hebrew mss in reading וְהַלֶּחֶם (vÿhallekhem, “and the bread”) rather than וּלְהַלֶּחֶם (ulÿhallekhem, “and to the bread”) of the Kethib. The syntax of the MT is confused here by the needless repetition of the preposition, probably taken from the preceding word.

12:1
ヘブライ語本文は難解である。マソラ本文（MT）のケチブ「私の悪行の上に」よりも、LXX（七十人訳）、シリア語ペシタ訳、ウルガタ訳に従って「私の苦しみのゆえに」と読むのがおそらく望ましい。ケチブの読みは目的語的な所有格（すなわち「自分に加えられた悪」）としては理解できるものの、この概念を表す通常のヘブライ語の慣用句には適合しない。また、マソラ本文のケレである「私の目」は、通常は「私の涙」を意味する換喩と解釈されるが、確かな読みとしては推奨されない。このヘブライ語句は「聖書学者（ソフェリム）の修正（ティクネ・ソフェリム）」の一つである。
[写本注] The Hebrew text is difficult here. It is probably preferable to read with the LXX, the Syriac Peshitta, and Vulgate בְּעוֹנִי (bÿ’onyi, “on my affliction”) rather than the Kethib of the MT בָּעַוֹנִי (ba’avoni, “on my wrongdoing”). While this Kethib reading is understandable as an objective genitive (i.e., “the wrong perpetrated upon me”), it does not conform to normal Hebrew idiom for this idea. The Qere of the MT בְּעֵינֵי (bÿ’eni, “on my eyes”), usually taken as synecdoche to mean “my tears,” does not commend itself as a likely meaning. The Hebrew word is one of the so-called tiqqune sopherim, or “emendations of the scribes.”
