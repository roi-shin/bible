1

After the plague the Lord said to Moses and to Eleazar son of Aaron the priest,
12
2

“Take a census of the whole community of Israelites, from twenty years old and upward, by their clans, everyone who can serve in the army of Israel.”
12

So Moses and Eleazar the priest spoke with them in the rift valley plains of Moab, along the Jordan River across from Jericho. They said,
12

“Number the people from twenty years old and upward, just as the Lord commanded Moses and the Israelites who went out from the land of Egypt.”
1

ルベンはイスラエルの長子である。ルベンの子孫：ハノクよりハノク氏族、パルよりパル氏族、
Reuben was the firstborn of Israel. The Reubenites: from Hanoch, the family of the Hanochites; from Pallu, the family of the Palluites;
1

from Hezron, the family of the Hezronites; from Carmi, the family of the Carmites.

These were the families of the Reubenites; and those numbered of them were 43,730.
1
8
パルの子孫はエリブ。
Pallu’s descendant was Eliab.
1
9

Eliab’s descendants were Nemuel, Dathan, and Abiram. It was Dathan and Abiram who as leaders of the community rebelled against Moses and Aaron with the followers of Korah when they rebelled against the Lord.
1
10

The earth opened its mouth and swallowed them and Korah at the time that company died, when the fire consumed 250 men. So they became a warning.
11
しかしコラの子孫は死ななかった。
But the descendants of Korah did not die.

To a larger group you will give a larger inheritance, and to a smaller group you will give a smaller inheritance. To each one its inheritance must be given according to the number of people in it.

For the Lord had said of them, “They will surely die in the wilderness.” And there was not left a single man of them, except Caleb son of Jephunneh and Joshua son of Nun.

NET Notes (翻訳者注)

1:1
[解説] アウトライン作成の目的における本章の区分は、本質的にイスラエルの部族に従うものとなる。書式や構造は第1回の人口調査に類似しているため、ここでの解説は少なくてすむ。
[解説] The breakdown of ch. for outlining purposes will be essentially according to the tribes of Israel. The format and structure is similar to the first census, and so less comment is necessary here.
1:2
[写本注] マソラ本文には「またこう言った」ともある。
[写本注] The MT has also “saying.”
2:1
[翻訳注] ヘブライ語では「その父たちの家」。
[翻訳注] Heb “house of their fathers.”
2:2
[翻訳注] ヘブライ語では「イスラエルにおいて軍隊に出て行くすべての者」。
[翻訳注] Heb “everyone who goes out in the army in Israel.”
3:1
[翻訳注] 「川」という言葉はヘブライ語本文にはないが、明確さのために翻訳において補われた（62節でも同様）。
[翻訳注] The word “River” is not in the Hebrew text, but has been supplied in the translation for clarity (also in v. 62).
3:2
[地図] エリコの場所についてはMap5-B2、Map6-E1、Map7-E1、Map8-E3、Map10-A2、Map11-A1を参照せよ。
[地図] For the location of Jericho see Map5-B2; Map6-E1; Map7-E1; Map8-E3; Map10-A2; Map11-A1.

[翻訳注] 読みを円滑にするため、「人々を数えよ」が本文に加えられている。
[翻訳注] “Number the people” is added here to the text for a smooth reading.
5:1
[写本注] ヘブライ語本文にはここに前置詞がないが、明確さのために翻訳で補われている。23、30、31、32節を参照せよ。

61:1
[翻訳注] エシュ・ザラー（“esh zarah、異なった火）という表現は不正確であり、数多くの方法で解釈されてきた（J. E. Hartley, Leviticus [WBC 4], 132-33の有益なまとめを参照せよ）。この違反には、以下の点、あるいはその組み合わせのいずれかが含まれていた可能性がある：(1) 燔祭の祭壇以外の場所からの炭を使用すること（すなわち、J. Milgrom, Leviticus [AB], 1:598による「許可されていない炭」；レビ記16:12および民数記16:40 [HT 17:5]の「権限のない者」[イシュ・ザル、'ish zar]、NASB「素人」を参照）、(2) 間違った種類の香を使用すること（香の祭壇上の「異なった香」[ケトレト・ザラー、qÿtoret zarah]に関する出エジプト記30章9節の規定、および出エジプト記30章34-38節との考えられる関連性を参照）、(3) 定められていない時に香の献げ物をすること（B. A. Levine, Leviticus [JPSTC], 59）、あるいは (4) 不適切な時に至聖所に入る事（レビ記16:1-2）。[解説] この出来事はレビ記10章1-7節に語られている。
[翻訳注] The expression אֵשׁ זָרָה (’esh zarah, “strange fire”) seems imprecise and has been interpreted numerous ways (see the helpful summary in J. E. Hartley, Leviticus [WBC 4], 132-33). The infraction may have involved any of the following or a combination thereof: (1) using coals from some place other than the burnt offering altar (i.e., “unauthorized coals” according to J. Milgrom, Leviticus [AB], 1:598; cf. Lev 16:12 and cf. “unauthorized person” [אִישׁ זָר, ’ish zar] in Num 16:40 [17:5 HT], NASB “layman”), (2) using the wrong kind of incense (cf. the Exod 30:9 regulation against “strange incense” [קְטֹרֶת זָרָה, qÿtoret zarah] on the incense altar and the possible connection to Exod 30:34-38), (3) performing an incense offering at an unprescribed time (B. A. Levine, Leviticus [JPSTC], 59), or (4) entering the Holy of Holies at an inappropriate time (Lev 16:1-2).[解説] This event is narrated in Lev 10:1-7.
